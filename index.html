Operating rules

  • Total metal = analysis × dry tonnes (dmt). Au/Ag/Pt/Pd/Rh in g/t, Cu in %.
  • Deduction = the greater of analysis × (1 − Yield) and Min. deduction. Payable grade = (analysis − deduction) × Metal payment %, never below 0.
  • Metal value = payable metal × live Kitco price (Bid, converted to €/kg).
  • Refining charge applies to the payable metal (€/kg or €/t of metal).
  • Treatment = rate × dry tonnes. Sampling = flat fee per lot, depending on the threshold.
  • Total amount = Σ metal value − Σ refining charges − treatment − sampling.
  • Pt and Rh had no rules in the Excel file — they were set the same as Pd; change them here if needed.
